Hermes privacy policy

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner.

It requests read access to the owner's Google account data (such as Gmail and Calendar) solely to prepare reminders and summaries for the owner. The data is processed on the owner's own computers, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
